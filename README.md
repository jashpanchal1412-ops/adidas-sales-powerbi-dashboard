# adidas-sales-powerbi-dashboard
Adidas Sales Dashboard 
Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Adidas sales performance across regions, retailers, products, and sales methods. It helps stakeholders track revenue, profit, and units sold, and identify which markets and products drive the most growth.

Key Features

KPI cards for Total Sales, Operating Profit, Units Sold, and Operating Margin
Sales and profit trends over time
Regional and state-level performance analysis
Retailer and product category comparisons
Interactive slicers for region, retailer, product, and sales method
Custom DAX measures for profit margin and year-over-year growth

Key Insights

[Region] generated the highest sales, contributing [X]% of total revenue
[Product] was the top-performing category by profit
[Retailer] led in total units sold
[Sales method, e.g., Online] showed the highest operating margin

Tools Used: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Excel
